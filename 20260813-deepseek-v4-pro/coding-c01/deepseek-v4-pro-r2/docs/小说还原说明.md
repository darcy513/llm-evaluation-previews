# 《极光》飞船还原说明与原文依据

## 一、资料检索过程

1. 通过 Internet Archive 找到《Aurora》（Kim Stanley Robinson, Orbit, 2015）公开扫描本
   （item: `aurora-by-kim-stanley-robinson`），下载并全文检索了 843,487 字符的 OCR 文本。
2. 交叉核对 Wikipedia 词条 *Aurora (novel)* 与 Centauri Dreams 的科学评论
   *A Science Critique of Aurora by Kim Stanley Robinson*（Stephen Baxter / James Benford / Joseph Miller），
   后者给出了船体几何的系统整理。
3. 依据小说 Chapter 2 中 Ship（飞船 AI）的自述，逐项提取尺寸与结构数据建模。

## 二、关键原文与模型对应

> Two thousand, one hundred twenty-two people are living in a multigenerational starship, headed for Tau Ceti, 11.9 light-years from Earth. The ship is made of two rings or toruses attached by spokes to a central spine. The spine is ten kilometers long. Each torus is made of twelve cylinders. Each cylinder is four kilometers long, and contains within it a particular specific Terran ecosystem.

→ 中央脊柱 10 km；两座环；每环 12 个圆柱舱；每舱 4 km。

> The biome cylinders are a kilometer in diameter, and four kilometers long. The tunnels between the biomes are usually left open… The biomes are filled lengthwise with land and lake surfaces. Their climates are configured to create analogs of the Terran ecosystems being modeled. There is a sunline running along the length of the ceiling of each biome. Ceilings are located on the sides of the rings nearest the spine. The rotation of the ship around its spinal axis creates a .83 g equivalent in the rings, pushing centrifugally outward, which inside the rings is then perceived as down, and the floors are therefore on that side. Under the biome floors, fuel, water, and other supplies are stored, which also creates shielding against cosmic rays.

→ 舱体直径 1 km；穹顶在朝脊柱一侧，地板在朝外一侧；地板下方有燃料/水屏蔽层；太阳线沿穹顶纵贯全舱。

> Ring B was only fifty-four kilometers around, so she would never be far away.

→ 每环周长约 54 km（12 × 4 km 舱体 + 舱间联锁段），模型采用中心半径约 8.4 km 的正十二边形环。

> …the tunnels are canted at fifteen-degree angles to the biomes at each end.

→ 相邻舱体夹角 30°，模型在两舱端部之间放置 15° 斜接的联锁隧道。

> The inner rings are structural-support rings connecting the six spokes near the spine. … “No, a hexagon,” Euan said. “There are six spokes, so the inner ring is a hexagon. The outer ones are a dodecahedron, but it’s less obvious because of the locks.”

→ 每环六根辐条；靠近脊柱处为六边形内环；外环为十二边形。

> During most of the voyage, this fuel was deployed as cladding around the toruses and spine. The deceleration is being accomplished by the frequent rapid fusion explosion of small pellets of deuterium/helium 3 fuel in a rocket engine at the bow of the ship. These explosions exert a retarding force on the ship equivalent to .005 g. The deceleration will therefore be complete in just under twenty years.

→ 脊柱与环上设置燃料包层；船艏设惯性约束聚变引擎；动画以 **每秒 2 发微爆** 表现减速（原文：“twenty years of fusion bombs going off twice a second”）。

> First, the twelve cylinders in each of the two toruses of the ship contain ecosystems modeling the twelve major Terran ecological zones… Ring A consists of twelve Old World ecosystems matching these categories, Ring B twelve New World ecosystems.

→ 12 个气候带双版本：Ring A（旧世界）/ Ring B（新世界）。

## 四、内部场景的文本来源

- **新斯科舍**：*“all of Nova Scotia is visible to them: forested hills around a lake… Long Pond…”* 模型包含森林丘陵、长池、帆船意象与小镇。
- **蒙古**：原文人物列表开篇 *“In Mongolia: Altan, Mongke…”*，以及 Freya 的旧世界环游路径；模型为金色草原与蒙古包聚落。
- **拉布拉多**：*“Labrador, with its taiga, glacier, estuary, and cold salt lake… town was made of cubical buildings with steep roofs, each one painted a bright primary color”*；模型包含冰川、冰封盐湖、针叶林与彩色房屋。
- **脊柱舰桥/观测舱**：辐条舷窗段落 *“Above them, the spine extended forward toward Tau Ceti…”*；AI 核心意象来自 Ship 的第一人称叙事。
- **聚变引擎舱**：*“turbine rooms next to the reactor”* 与减速引擎描述；模型包含反应堆、涡轮与每秒 2 发燃料微丸。
- **六号辐条/内环**：*“Spoke Six, where a spiral staircase on the inner wall of the spoke took them up to Inner Ring B”*；模型包含螺旋梯、舷窗与六边形内环室。

## 五、如实说明的局限性

1. 小说没有给脊柱直径、辐条直径、引擎外形等具体尺寸，这些细节为满足 10 km 脊柱 + 1 km 舱体比例的合理工程补全。
2. 原著没有随文插图；本模型是三维重建，不是官方设定图。
3. 两环是否反向旋转原文未直接说明（双环结构通常反向抵消角动量），模型采用反向旋转并保留转速微变化。
4. OCR 文本中的小写/断行误差已在引用时修正，不影响数据提取。
